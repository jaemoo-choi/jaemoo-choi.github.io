Post-Training, Distillation, and Test-Time Scaling for Diffusion Models
Reward fine-tuning and long-horizon generation for image, video, and world models.
  • Likelihood estimation Rethinking RL · ICML 2026
    The ELBO likelihood estimator, not the RL loss, drives diffusion RL: GenEval 0.24 → 0.95 on SD3.5-M, 4.6× more efficient than FlowGRPO.
  • Scalable RL RVM · Preprint 2026 · with NVIDIA
    Folds reward feedback into a trajectory-free, likelihood-free velocity-matching loss that recovers Reinforce Adjoint Matching and stays close to ELBO-based RL; best VBench on Wan2.1-T2V.
  • Long-horizon distillation LongTake · Preprint 2026
    Trains with long-horizon teacher forcing, then initializes DMD directly, skipping few-step distillation; more motion over 30‑second rollouts.
  • Test-time scaling In-Distribution Forcing · Preprint 2026
    Self-caches at test time to keep KV entries in distribution, extending short-horizon models to minute-long video.
Diffusion Samplers for Scientific Domains through Stochastic Optimal Control
Adjoint Schrödinger bridge samplers for Boltzmann distributions, and their extensions.
  • Boltzmann sampling ASBS · NeurIPS 2025 · Oral 0.3%
    Generalizes adjoint sampling to the Schrödinger bridge, relaxing the memoryless condition. Provably convergent, it scales to molecular conformers.
    • Annealed dynamics NAAS · NeurIPS 2025
      Replaces the uninformative reference process with non-equilibrium annealed dynamics, learned through an adjoint-matching-style lean adjoint system.
    • Mode coverage PDNS · ICLR 2026
      Breaks sampling into proximal steps on path measures, each trained with a cross-entropy loss, to curb mode collapse.
    • Discrete spaces DASBS · ICML 2026
      Carries adjoint matching and ASBS to discrete state spaces, where no gradients exist.
