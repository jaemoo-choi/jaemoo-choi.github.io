<p></p>

<p>Hi! I’m Jaemoo Choi, a postdoctoral researcher in the FLAIR Lab at <strong>Georgia Tech</strong>, mentored by Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=2z7iDDUAAAAJ&hl" target="_blank">Yongxin Chen</a>.</p>

<p>My research focuses on <strong>fundamental algorithms for <span class="kw-model">diffusion and flow models</span></strong>, grounded in stochastic optimal control and optimal transport, to make them more <strong>controllable, efficient, and reliable</strong>. I develop <strong class="kw-method">post-training, distillation, test-time scaling, and sampling methods</strong> for <strong class="kw-app">image, video, and world models</strong>, as well as <strong class="kw-app">diffusion samplers for scientific domains</strong> such as molecular systems. Recently, I have been especially interested in <strong class="kw-app">long-horizon video and world models</strong> for <strong class="kw-app">Physical AI</strong>.</p>

<p>Previously, I was jointly affiliated with the Graduate School of Data Science at Seoul National University, where I worked with Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=M-MfqpMAAAAJ&hl" target="_blank">Joonseok Lee</a> and his group. I received my <strong>Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences</strong> and B.S. in Mathematics Education from Seoul National University.</p>

<p><strong class="text-primary">I’m actively seeking full-time opportunities.</strong></p>
